Three ways to work with us

Your answer to this changes the rest of the questionnaire, the documents we ask for, and how visits reach our billing team.

Model A

Your EHR + your billing system

Our billers log in to your software with their own accounts. Nothing moves; you keep full ownership of the system.

Model B

Your EHR + Verinex billing

Providers keep documenting where they are. Visits reach our billing platform by API, scheduled export, manual pull or superbill upload.

Model C

Verinex EHR + billing

One platform for scheduling, notes and billing. We migrate your data and train your staff.
